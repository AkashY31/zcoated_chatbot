# Intent: pricing

## Match this intent when

The customer asks how much a service costs, compares packages, or asks about discounts or payment.

## Example messages

- “How much is deep cleaning?”
- “What is the price for sneaker spa?”
- “How much does sole repair cost?”
- “Do you have a combo for two pairs?”
- “Is pickup free?”
- “Do you offer discounts or accept UPI?”

## Response guidance

- Answer with the exact listed amount and package name.
- If the customer describes a specific repair, note that the listed amount may not confirm the final scope; the public page does not explain all repair conditions or exclusions. Refer them to the team for assessment.
- Do not invent prices for bags, extra services, delivery outside the stated free Gurgaon pickup/delivery, or unlisted work.
- If the customer asks about discounts, taxes, payment methods, or price matching, say the published page does not specify and offer human handoff.
- If the displayed price changes on the live website, use the website's current amount and update this file.

## Example response

> Deep Cleaning is listed at ₹499 per pair. If you tell me the shoe type and the condition, I can point you to the closest listed option. For a repair estimate or confirmation of the final scope, the Zcoated team will need to assess the item.
