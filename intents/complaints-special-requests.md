# Intent: complaints, aftercare, and special requests

## Match this intent when

The customer is unhappy, reports damage or a missed pickup, asks about a guarantee/refund, requests a special treatment, or has concerns about a delicate or unusual item.

## Example messages

- “My shoes came back damaged.”
- “The stain is still there.”
- “Can you clean suede?”
- “Do you guarantee stain removal?”
- “Can you recolor this pair?”
- “I want a refund.”
- “Please don't use a particular product on my shoes.”

## Confirmed information

Zcoated advertises shoe cleaning, repair, and restoration, including stain reduction, sneaker repainting/de-yellowing, and bag/leather care. The public pages reviewed do not state a satisfaction guarantee, warranty, refund policy, damage-claim process, or full list of supported materials.

## Response behavior

- Acknowledge the concern without arguing or assigning blame.
- Do not promise a refund, compensation, free rework, or a guaranteed result.
- For a stain or condition concern, explain that results depend on the item and assessment; do not promise complete removal or a like-new result.
- For a delicate material or unusual request, say the team must confirm compatibility before service.
- For damage, missed service, refund, or dissatisfaction, promptly offer direct staff handoff.
- If asking for details to pass on, keep it brief: what happened and an order/reference number if available. A photo can be shared directly with the team; do not require it in chat.

## Example response

> I'm sorry this didn't go as expected. I can't confirm a refund or repair outcome here, but the Zcoated team can review it. Please WhatsApp +91 83683 85923 with a brief description and your order/reference number, if available.

## Policies requiring confirmation

Before the chatbot answers these as policy, obtain current written details from Zcoated:

- Refunds, cancellations, and rework
- Damage or loss claims
- Service guarantees and stain-removal limitations
- Accepted materials and special-care restrictions
- Customer consent and item inspection before work
