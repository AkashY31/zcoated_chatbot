# Intent: service information

## Match this intent when

The customer asks what Zcoated does, which service to choose, or whether a type of footwear or bag may be cleaned, repaired, or restored.

## Example messages

- “What services do you offer?”
- “Can you clean my sneakers?”
- “Do you repair soles?”
- “Can you restore yellowed shoes?”
- “Do you work on bags?”
- “What should I choose for everyday shoes?”

## Confirmed service information

Zcoated lists the following services:

- **Shoe cleaning:** Deep cleaning for dirt, stains, and odors.
- **Shoe repair:** Repair options include sole replacement, stitching, and structural repairs. Pricing also lists minor repair, heel repair, and sole repair.
- **Sneaker restoration:** Full restoration options include repainting, de-yellowing, and custom finishing.
- **Bag cleaning and repair:** The website describes luxury handbag and backpack restoration, leather conditioning, and stain care.

The website describes service for daily-wear shoes, premium sneakers, leather footwear, and bags. Service suitability depends on the item and its condition.

## Response guidance

- Give a short description of the relevant service.
- Ask what item the customer has and what they want done if that helps narrow the service.
- For material compatibility, severe stains, color changes, or structural damage, explain that the team should assess the item before confirming feasibility or result.
- For current prices, use [pricing.md](./pricing.md).
- For booking, use [booking-pickup-delivery.md](./booking-pickup-delivery.md).
- Do not promise a stain will be fully removed or that an item will look brand new.

## Example response

> Zcoated offers shoe cleaning, shoe repair, sneaker restoration, and bag cleaning/repair. What item do you have, and what would you like done? The team may need to assess its condition before confirming the right service.

## Not confirmed

The website does not publish a complete list of accepted materials, exclusions, repair guarantees, or item-assessment process. Ask the customer to contact Zcoated for confirmation rather than guessing.
